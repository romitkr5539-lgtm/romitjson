About

Built for fast, private JSON work.

RomitJSON gives you a clean browser-based toolbox for formatting, validating, comparing, converting, and generating JSON data without sending your files anywhere.

ϟ

Local by design

Everything runs in the browser. Your JSON stays on your device while you format, validate, compare, or convert it.

♙

Made for productivity

Move from raw JSON to readable output, clean CSV, YAML, XML, or TypeScript declarations in seconds.

♧

No signup required

Open the tool, paste or load a file, and start working immediately. There is no account or subscription flow to slow you down.

Simple tools for real JSON tasks.

JSON is the backbone of modern apps, APIs, and config files. Whether you are debugging a payload, cleaning data, or generating code, the right tool should feel fast, clear, and non-intrusive.

RomitJSON combines a compact set of helpers that are easy to use from any browser and reliable enough for everyday work. The goal is to keep the process tidy, local, and transparent.

What the project focuses on.

Privacy first

Files are read locally and never uploaded to a server.

Fast feedback

Formatting, validation, and conversion are immediate and easy to review.

Clear output

Generated results are ready to copy, download, and share without extra cleanup.

Tooling breadth

From compare and viewer tools to TypeScript generation and XML conversion, the suite covers common JSON workflow tasks.
